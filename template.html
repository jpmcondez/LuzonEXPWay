Fares

When the TRB publishes new rates: open its toll-rate page, copy one class's table, choose Paste TRB matrix, paste, and save. Then download fares.csv and commit it. Fares are stored per toll system, by entry and exit. Where a trip passes from one system into another, the handover point is called “Other system junction”. When a fare is missing, type it into the breakdown and it’s saved in this browser. To publish your fares, download fares.csv and commit it next to index.html.

Columns: system,from,to,class1,class2,class3,note. A fare applies both ways. Use * for “any” — e.g. NAIAX,*,*,… for one flat toll on a road. Plaza names are the ones on the map without the “(road)” suffix. Systems: .
Paste a toll matrix
Copy one class's table from the TRB page (trb.gov.ph → Toll Rates), or from a spreadsheet, and paste it below. The first row should list entry plazas and the first column exit plazas. Tabs or commas both work, and blank or “–” cells are skipped. Fares are saved for both directions. Names are matched to map plazas through the plaza list; any unmatched names are listed before saving.
