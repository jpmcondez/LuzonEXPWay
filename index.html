Fares

Fares are stored per toll system, by entry and exit. Where a trip passes from one system into another, the handover point is called “Other system junction”. When a fare is missing, type it into the breakdown and it’s saved in this browser. To publish your fares, download fares.csv and commit it next to index.html.
